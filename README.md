# Parley for Homebrew

Parley turns what you say into text in any app on your Mac: double-tap Fn, talk, tap Fn, and the cleaned-up text
appears at your cursor.

```sh
brew install --cask donvaughn/parley-tap/parley
```

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later. Update with `brew upgrade --cask parley`. Remove with
`brew uninstall --cask parley`; add `--zap` to remove your history, dictionary and the speech models as well.
